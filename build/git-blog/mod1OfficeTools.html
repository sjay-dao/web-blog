Module 1: Introduction to Office Productivity Tools

💡 What is Office Software?

Office software refers to programs used to create documents, presentations, spreadsheets, and more. These tools help with:

  • Writing and editing documents
  • Creating presentations
  • Organizing data using spreadsheets
  • Managing email and calendars
  • Collaborating in real time

Examples of Office Software:

  • Microsoft Office: Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook
  • Google Workspace: Docs, Sheets, Slides, Gmail

🔄 Microsoft Office vs Google Workspace

Feature Microsoft Office Google Workspace
Access Installed on computer (desktop-based) Web-based (works on any browser)
Collaboration Limited real-time editing Real-time collaboration
Storage Files stored locally or in OneDrive Files saved automatically to Google Drive
Cost Paid (subscription or one-time purchase) Free with Google account (premium available)
Offline Use Works offline Offline mode available but needs setup

🔟 Creating a Google Account

If you don’t already have a Google account, now’s a good time to create one. A Google account gives you access to:

  • Gmail (email)
  • Google Drive (cloud storage)
  • Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides
  • Google Calendar
  • And much more!

Steps to create a Google Account:

  1. Go to accounts.google.com/signup
  2. Fill in your name and preferred email
  3. Create a strong password
  4. Follow the on-screen instructions to finish setup

✅ Summary

  • Office software helps with productivity and organization.
  • Microsoft Office and Google Workspace are the most popular options.
  • Google Workspace is ideal for collaboration and accessibility.
  • Create a Google account to start using Google’s free office tools.
